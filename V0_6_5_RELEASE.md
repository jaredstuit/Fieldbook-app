# Fieldbook v0.6.5

Usability and foundation update.

## Included
- Saved field boundaries restored on Map & Soils.
- Pacific Time handling for field notes and observations.
- Dedicated Overview, Timeline, Samples, Notes & Observations, and Map & Soils pages.
- Lighter Overview queries for faster field navigation.
- Edit screens for growers, ranches, fields, notes, observations, and samples.
- Crop and irrigation autocomplete with custom entries allowed.
- Water-source selection on field creation/editing.

No new Supabase SQL or environment-variable changes are required.
